# WISERD Brand Guidelines

## Brand Essence
- **Brand Name:** WISERD
- **Category:** Personal Intelligence Platform
- **Tagline:** Understand more. Stay ahead.
- **Core Proposition:** Open WISERD. See what changed. Understand why it matters.
- **Global Time Standard:** UTC

## Brand Voice
- **Tone:** Editorial, authoritative, lucid, concise, high signal.
- **Style Rules:** Fluent natural English only. Zero em dashes or decorative hyphens in headlines and UI copy. Zero bracketed robotic tags. Separate verified facts from analytical interpretation at all times.

## Color Architecture
- **Obsidian Void:** `#060608`
- **Elevated Surface:** `#0D0F16`
- **Studio Vermilion:** `#FF4814`
- **Optical Cobalt:** `#0047FF`
- **Signal Amber:** `#F59E0B`
- **Verified Emerald:** `#059669`
- **Alabaster Stage:** `#F3F2EE`

## Typography
- **Display & UI Sans:** Plus Jakarta Sans (500, 600, 700, 800)
- **Editorial Accent Serif:** Instrument Serif Italic (400)
- **Tabular & Technical:** JetBrains Mono (500, 700)

## Iconography Standard
- Dual layer 3D Frosted Glassmorphism SVG icons combining saturated cobalt/vermilion/amber/emerald back geometry, Gaussian optical refraction bleed, curved top glass sheen, and crisp white foreground glyphs.
